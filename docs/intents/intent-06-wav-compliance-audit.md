TLC are auditing our wheelchair-accessible service commitments. Notice came
through this morning; they want to see records covering the first half of the
year and the window is six weeks.

What they've asked for, roughly: evidence that the accessible capacity we're
licensed for is actually in service and being used, broken down in a way they
can interrogate. In past audits with other operators they've wanted it by
location and by period, and they've pushed back hard when an operator could only
produce a fleet-wide total.

I don't know exactly which cut they'll ask for on the day, which is the
uncomfortable part. I'd rather we could answer several variations than build one
number and hope it's the right one.

The thing I want to avoid is being unable to say something because we didn't
keep it or can't get at it. If there's a gap, I need to know now rather than in
week five.
