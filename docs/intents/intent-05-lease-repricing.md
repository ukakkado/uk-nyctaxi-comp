# Intent 05

**From:** Priya Raghunathan, Commercial Director
**To:** Data Platform
**Date:** 2024-07-22
**Needed by:** mid-September, before the lease committee meets

---

We're repricing the lease book in the autumn and I want to go in better informed
than last time.

The question I keep returning to: what do our drivers actually earn, and how
wide is the spread? My instinct is the flat daily rate is doing something odd at
both ends — leaving money on the table with the strongest drivers and squeezing
the weakest to the point where they leave us. That is instinct, though, and I'd
like to know whether it survives contact with the numbers.

What you have to work with, as I understand it:

- Three engagement types on the book: daily lease, weekly lease, and
  owner-operator on a revenue share.
- agg.agg_driver_monthly and ops.mart_driver_scorecard already exist. I don't
  know whether they answer this or not.
- January to June is the period I care about. Anything older predates the
  current rate card.

I'm not after a dashboard and I don't want another report nobody opens. Write me
something I can read, tell me what's true, and tell me how much confidence to put
in it.

If the honest answer is that the data can't support the question, I would much
rather hear that than get a number with a shrug attached to it.
