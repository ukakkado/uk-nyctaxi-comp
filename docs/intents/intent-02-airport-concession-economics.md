Port Authority want to reopen the concession terms at LaGuardia and JFK. First
session is provisional for early September.

I need to walk into that room knowing what an airport pickup is genuinely worth
to us. Not the fare — the whole thing, everything that lands on the trip. And
not an average across six months either, because their argument will be about
the hours when the ranks are congested and mine needs to be about the same
hours.

What would help most is something showing how the economics move across the day,
and by where the trip's actually going, because a run into midtown and a run out
to the far end of Queens are not the same business and I don't want to defend an
average of the two.

One thing I'd flag: the last time someone pulled numbers on this we ended up
arguing internally about what counted, and the meeting went nowhere. I'd rather
settle that with you up front than in front of them.
