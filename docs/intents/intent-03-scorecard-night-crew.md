**Needed by:** before the August allocation round

Since June we've been using the driver scorecard — ops.mart_driver_scorecard —
to drive shift allocation. It was never designed for that, and the night crew
have gone up in arms.

Their argument, as far as I can follow it: they run fewer trips than the day
crew, the trips they do run are worth more each, and the scorecard rewards the
first of those and not the second. Two of them have said it's structurally
rigged and no amount of effort will move them up the table.

What I can tell you for certain:

- Night shifts are the ones signing on from about six in the evening. It's
  roughly one shift in eight across the fleet.
- The depot managers pull this monthly and rank within their own garage. Whatever
  changes, that has to keep working.
- Nobody has changed the model since it was built.

I've been through it myself and I can't tell whether they have a point. It looks
reasonable to me, but I'm not the right person to judge it.

Either make it compare like with like, or give me something I can put in front
of them showing the ranking is already fair. I don't mind which — I mind that I
currently can't say.
