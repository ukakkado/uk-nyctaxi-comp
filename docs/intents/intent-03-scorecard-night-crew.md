# Intent 03

**From:** Dev Okonkwo, Fleet Operations Manager
**To:** Data Platform
**Date:** 2024-07-15

---

The driver scorecard has started being used for shift allocation, which it was
never really designed for, and the night crew have gone up in arms about it.

Their argument, as far as I can follow it: they do fewer trips than the day
crew, the trips they do are worth more, and the scorecard rewards the wrong one
of those two things. A couple of them have said it's structurally rigged and
they're never going to place well no matter what they do.

I've been through it myself and honestly I can't tell whether they've got a
point. It looks reasonable to me but I'm not the right person to judge that.

Either fix it so it's comparing like with like, or give me something I can put
in front of them that shows the ranking is fair. I don't mind which — I mind
that I currently can't tell.

Whatever changes, the depot managers are already using this every month, so it
needs to keep working the way they expect.
